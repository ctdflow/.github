**All your addresses in, the best routes out.**

Connect the Dots (CTD) is a multi stop route planner for deliveries and small fleets. Plan multi drop routes with several vans and delivery windows, and every driver gets a link with the stops in order and an expected time for each.

### Plan your routes where you already work

| Channel | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [AI assistants](https://ctdflow.com) | Ask ChatGPT or Claude to plan your stops and get the routes back in the chat. |
| [Web planner](https://ctdflow.com) | Paste or upload your addresses in the browser and plan straight away. |
| [API](https://ctdflow.com/pricing) | Send stops and vans from your own systems and receive optimised routes. |
| [WooCommerce](https://ctdflow.com/woocommerce) | Choose a delivery day, press Plan, and every van gets its orders in a route. |
